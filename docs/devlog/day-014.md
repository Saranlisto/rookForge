# Day 014 - En Passant

## Summary

Added en passant generation and application. En passant remains represented by the existing `Move` type: the capturing pawn moves to the FEN en passant target square, and `apply_move` removes the captured pawn behind that target.

## Implemented

- En passant target squares continue to be set after double pawn pushes.
- Non-double-pawn moves clear the en passant target.
- Pawn move generation now includes en passant captures when:
  - the FEN en passant target is present,
  - the target is empty,
  - the capturing pawn can move diagonally onto the target,
  - the captured pawn exists behind the target square.
- `apply_move` now removes the captured pawn for en passant.
- En passant resets the halfmove clock.
- En passant clears the en passant target square.
- Legal move filtering rejects en passant captures that expose the moving side's king.

## Tests Added

- Non-double-pawn move clears en passant target
- White en passant generation
- Black en passant generation
- White en passant removes captured pawn
- Black en passant removes captured pawn
- En passant resets halfmove clock
- En passant clears en passant target
- No en passant without target square
- No en passant for the wrong side to move
- Illegal en passant discovered-check case is filtered out
- CLI integration coverage for en passant legal moves and apply output

## Local Checks

The local smoke workflow now includes:

```bash
cargo run -- movegen legal --fen "8/8/8/3pP3/8/8/8/4K2k w - d6 0 1"
cargo run -- apply --fen "8/8/8/3pP3/8/8/8/4K2k w - d6 0 1" --move e5d6
```

Run the complete validation workflow before committing:

```bash
make check
```
